Ask your tutor for practice links

Try one of these requests with a teacher or AI assistant. Include this guide so they can follow the link format.

Teach me how to write 你好. Explain the meaning, give me a Hanzi Guide practice link with Mandarin pronunciation and simplified characters, and suggest a short writing exercise.
I’m learning Cantonese with traditional characters. Give me three useful words, each no longer than six Hanzi, with meanings, Jyutping, and a Hanzi Guide practice link. Use https://www.hanzi.guide/tutor/ for the link format.

Check unfamiliar readings and explanations with a trusted teacher or dictionary. An AI assistant may need you to supply the guide link; providing this page does not guarantee that it can operate the interactive controls.

Exercise links for tutors and assistants

A practice link supplies the characters, pronunciation language, and character form:

{{ site.url }}/?pronunciation=mandarin&script=simplified#%E4%BD%A0%E5%A5%BD

That example opens 你好 with Mandarin pronunciation and simplified characters. Percent-encode the Hanzi after #; a browser may display the decoded characters in its address bar.

Supported practice-link fields
FieldSupported values
pronunciationmandarin or cantonese. Shows Pinyin or Jyutping and the matching listening controls.
scriptsimplified or traditional. Selects the character form in the practice tool.
Fragment after #A short group of Hanzi, up to the practice input’s six-character limit. Remove punctuation and split longer sentences into words or short groups.

Explicit language and character-form choices take precedence over saved display preferences. Once the exercise opens, use Play, a listening button, or I want to write! to begin the activity.

For a worksheet, use {{ site.url }}/pdf.html#%E4%BD%A0%E5%A5%BD. The PDF uses the characters exactly as supplied; the practice link’s language and character-form fields do not configure the PDF.

For longer sentences, provide several short practice links and keep the complete sentence in the lesson. Character readings are displayed individually; use the phrase listening button and your tutor’s explanation to check connected pronunciation.
